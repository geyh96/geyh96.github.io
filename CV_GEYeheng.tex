\section{\bf Summary }
\noindent 
My research lies at the intersection of statistical inference and machine learning.
I work in three main areas:
\begin{itemize}
	\item 
 Representation learning for machine learning tasks, including transfer learning
   and simulation-based inference for scientific modeling.
	\item 
 Streaming data analysis.
   My recent work focuses on stochastic gradient descent (SGD) type algorithms
   and their applications to statistical inference and privacy-preserving
   estimation for DNN-based models.
   	\item 
 Hypothesis testing and statistical inference in large language model applications,
   particularly membership inference attacks and related privacy problems.
\end{itemize}
